What it does

  • Animated rallyA perspective-projected court with animated players, serves, rushes, cross-court shots, and a live scoreboard.
  • Singles or doublesPick your format in the Options sheet. Doubles rallies use the three-number score call with both partners serving.
  • Black-light themeSwitch from the classic court to a neon-on-black look, glow and all, right from the Options sheet.
  • Live weatherCurrent conditions for your city with a “good day to play?” badge, powered by Open-Meteo. No account needed.
  • Nearby tournamentsUpcoming tournaments near your city across 25 US metros, refreshed hourly.
  • Drill of the dayOne practice drill each day, filterable by DUPR tier (3.0–5.0) or all levels.
  • Widget railClock, weather, tournaments, and drill cards — each one toggleable in the Options sheet.
  • Native & lightweightA universal binary drawn with CoreGraphics. Signed and notarized, so macOS installs it without warnings.

Install

  1. Download and open PickleballScreensaver.dmg.
  2. Double-click the installer package inside and follow the prompts. It installs for all users, so macOS asks for your password.
  3. Open System Settings → Screen Saver and pick PickleballScreensaver.
  4. Click Options… to turn on weather, tournaments, and the drill of the day, and to pick singles or doubles and the black-light theme.

Doesn't appear in the list? Quit and reopen System Settings — macOS caches the screensaver list. To uninstall, delete PickleballScreensaver.saver from /Library/Screen Savers.
